## Combined sleep and base trace
Rechecked actual bytecode of CarmaEvent6Procedure, SleepHandler and PlaceRedstoneTorchEvent. The God records bed coordinates and reacts to karma <50 or >50 (1/10 call); TBS checks nearby hostile anomalies (range 512) to deny sleep, and places redstone torches at a learned base. Our combination centers karma at zero, checks the active private watcher, preserves vanilla sleep errors, limits supernatural denials to one per 180 seconds (retry allowed), persists bed coordinates, and places a real redstone torch only into air on suitable ground near that bed, in loaded chunks behind the player. Bed is a proxy for learned base, not an exact port of PlayerBase. No original text or assets copied. From The Fog (https://www.lunareclipse.studio/creations/from-the-fog) is an additional design reference for subtle environmental traces and stalking, not yet ported.

## Watcher / Circuit reaction integration
Reopened both uploaded JARs and inspected javap -p -c output for WatcherPriObnovlieniiTikaSushchnostiProcedure, FollowerPriObnovlieniiTikaSushchnostiProcedure, CircuitDefaultStareBehavior, CircuitStalkBehavior and CircuitBlockBreakHandler. God watcher procedure decrements watchertimer and discards at zero; threshold 300, reset 900. Circuit stalk finds player within 256, delegates to stare behavior, then checks distance <=8 and LOS with random branch; applies blindness and chooses fake midnight or real Circuit spawn. Stare checks within 100, hitbox gaze and LOS, then branches to overlay/sound/despawn or real Circuit/darkness. Our shared watcher uses 300 ticks, 8-block stalk proximity, a 12-tick gaze debounce and block raycast; close stalk causes 100 ticks of blindness, spotted stalk causes 60 ticks of darkness, followed by removal. Ordinary watcher simply disappears. Vanilla sound and enderman placeholder remain; no real Circuit chase or fake midnight yet. We deliberately avoid the God's global effect clear/stopsound commands. CircuitBlockBreakHandler specifically triggers a configurable rare ore-disguised spawn when Null is present, not generic mining noise; exact disguise/chase and Null prerequisites are pending. New behavior is an adaptation, not a byte-for-byte port.

## Null and ore dependency
Direct javap inspection: CircuitBlockBreakHandler checks the ore platform tag, a random percentage against getDisguisedCircuitOreChance(), and MapVariables.isNullHere(), then creates a fast-spawn Circuit at the mined position. EntityConfig default ore chance is 0.01%; entity-death chance 0.001%. NullEvent checks isNullHere, survival and excludes the Null profile. NullIsNearEvent plays a custom sound and applies blindness for 120 ticks. NullWhisperEvent sends a gray italic incoming-message component with Null's profile name and randomized text. Our adaptation keeps the ore tag, default probability, six-second blindness and targeted whisper presentation. Null presence is per-player persistent and starts when the stage-4 director first selects NULL_NEAR; original world-global story arrival is not ported. Ore reveal is a client-only ore afterimage at the mined position, followed by existing stalk apparition (if placement succeeds); true disguised entity, original custom sounds, death disguise and full story are pending. New localized text is original. No real ore is replaced or drops modified.

## Real chase adapter
Direct javap inspection in this iteration: CircuitChaseGoal (canUse, start, tick, navigation) has a default detection range of 64, line-of-sight tracking, lastKnownPos and path navigation; FollowerEntity registers hurt-by and nearest-attackable target goals. The new adapter uses native mob navigation toward the target or last seen position, range 64 and 1.2 navigation speed. It starts on a one-in-three stalk reaction (our provisional probability), expires after 600 ticks or 100 ticks without LOS (our provisional limits), restricts targeting to the selected player and cancels item drops. Vanilla husk is a temporary proxy, with its native combat behavior, visible to nearby players; custom rendering, actual Circuit state machine and climbing remain pending. The 26.3 BreakBlockEvent fix is based on NeoForge source. No runtime test yet.
